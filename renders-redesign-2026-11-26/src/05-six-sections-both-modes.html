On the floor, always both modes

Who is here · Waiting (every ask, one queue) · On now (music, weather, hour, TV) · Damage / Heal by a typed number · the deck pill. Without the deck the floor also carries Frame and Manual.

How this differs from October's deck mode

October's version only hid buttons, and after you kept Camera, Party and Combat it hid nothing. This one regroups first (worth having with no deck at all), follows whether the deck is reachable, keeps every folded group one tap away, and comes back by itself the moment the deck drops.
