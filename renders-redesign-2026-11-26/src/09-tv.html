Two rough edges this removes

An intro leaves the game paused and nothing says so (37 of the 94 do). On now shows "Paused by an intro" with Resume.

The TV's volume sliders are reset on every launch while the panel still shows them as yours to set. The sliders say "resets on launch" beside them, with the switch.
