### 5. ⚠️ The conductor's punch has never been audible on any phone — fixed

The one piece of feedback that tells a player the ticket was taken and they are aboard.
`cmTicketFx.stop()` runs on the owning client — the phone — and called `punchSound()`, which called

```js

  const a = game.audio;
  if (!a || a.locked || !a.environment?.gainNode) return null;

}
```

And a phone zeroes that bus — `main.js:733-752`, §20.6, *"the TV is the room's speaker"*:
`globalPlaylistVolume` and `globalAmbientVolume` to 0, `globalInterfaceVolume` left alone. So the

iPhone the ticket tore away in complete silence.

**Fixed.** `audioOut(which = "environment")` is parameterised and `punchSound()` asks for
`"interface"` — the channel the phone-silencing rule deliberately leaves alone, which is the reason
the boss roar already rides it. Verified: the four remaining callers (the fiddle, the approach bed,
the whistle, the grinding stop) all take the default and all gate on `isPhoneClient()` first, so they
are table cues on the table's channel, unchanged.
