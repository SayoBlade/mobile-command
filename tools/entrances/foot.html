The sounds are built in your browser from tones and noise, so they only sketch the real thing. In Mobile Command each cue would be a file from your library, played at the same moment.

Portraits: the Crooked Moon module already has art for every NPC on this page, including before-and-after pictures for the Abbot and for Yorgrim and the Reaper, so the portrait slots could fill themselves. The silhouettes here are placeholders.

The book pairs most of these NPCs with a song from The Blasting Company's companion albums. They aren't in your library yet; if you get them, an entrance could end on its NPC's song.

Fonts: Cinzel and UnifrakturMaguntia already ship with Mobile Command. IM Fell English, Sancreek, Sacramento, Rye, Eater, Pirata One, Pinyon Script, Oldenburg, Cinzel Decorative and Uncial Antiqua would be new; all are under the SIL Open Font License.
