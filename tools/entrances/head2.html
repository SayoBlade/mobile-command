Crooked Moon Entrances II

Twenty-five entrance banners in the Boss Splash style. Six are transformations: the Crimson Abbot, the Chained Reaper, Golub and the Abbot's three priors change in front of the party. The map dims and a banner slides in with the NPC's own art from the book. It holds for about six seconds with a slow drift while everyone reads the name, then slides away. Each plays over its chapter's battle map, with recorded sounds from your library.

Your device asks for reduced motion, so the banners fade in and out instead of sliding.
%%CARDS%%

Art: The Crooked Moon module's own portraits, boss art, margin drawings and battle maps. Sound: recordings from your library, trimmed to fit; each card names its sounds.

Mist: MattM's Animated Mist and Fog, from your modules (thin horizontal mist for the Vagrant; a thick drifting fog bank and thin rising fog for the Reaper).

This page carries the book's art, your purchased sounds and that module's mist, so keep it private.

Full screen: the button on any banner, or Full screen at the top. Inside, the arrow keys step through the banners, Space plays or stops, and Esc leaves.

Fonts: Cinzel, UnifrakturMaguntia, Grenze Gotisch and Metamorphous already ship with Mobile Command. Limelight, Poiret One, Uncial Antiqua, IM Fell English, Sancreek, Cinzel Decorative, Sacramento, Eater, Pirata One, Pinyon Script, Rye and MedievalSharp would be new; all are under the SIL Open Font License.
